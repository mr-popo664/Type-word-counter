1. Why Indian Form Portals Reject Files That Look Fine on Your Phone

Every year, lakhs of candidates applying for Staff Selection Commission (SSC), Union Public Service Commission (UPSC), National Testing Agency (NTA NEET/JEE/CUET), Railway Recruitment Board (RRB), MPSC, and state scholarship portals like MahaDBT encounter cryptic upload errors such as “Invalid File Dimension,” “File size must be between 10 KB and 20 KB,” or “DPI mismatch.”

Unlike consumer social media platforms that accept any image and re-encode it on cloud servers, government form portals validate raw file headers directly in the browser and on NIC/TCS servers before saving your application. Four technical checks cause over 90% of rejections:

  • Strict Minimum KB Floors: Portals enforce a closed interval [Min KB, Max KB]. A crisp black-ink signature on white paper often compresses down to 6 KB or 7 KB at high JPEG quality, falling below SSC’s 10 KB floor or UPSC’s 20 KB floor.
  • Binary KiB (1024 Bytes) vs. Decimal KB (1000 Bytes): Windows Explorer and server upload validators divide byte length by 1024. An image measuring 50,800 bytes appears as 50.8 KB in decimal tools and exceeds a strict 50 KiB (51,200-byte) cap if metadata pushes it over.
  • 72 DPI Canvas Exports vs. 200/300 DPI Requirements: Standard mobile editors and browser canvases write 72 or 96 DPI into the JPEG APP0 JFIF header. Portals that inspect physical print density require 200 DPI (SSC, NTA, RRB) or 300 DPI (UPSC, PAN).
  • Shadowed Grey Paper Backgrounds: Photographing a signature under indoor LED lighting turns white paper into #D4D8DF grey, reducing ink-to-paper contrast below automated OCR thresholds.

3. UPSC 3× Vertical Signature & SSC OTR Rules in Detail

Two rules trip up first-time applicants more than any others. First, under SSC’s One-Time Registration (OTR) workflow, candidate photographs are captured live via webcam/mobile camera, while the signature must still be uploaded manually as a horizontal 480 × 160 px JPEG strictly inside 10 KB to 20 KB at 200 DPI.

Second, UPSC requires candidates to sign three times vertically (one signature stacked above the next) inside a square 400 × 400 px canvas at 300 DPI, sized between 20 KB and 100 KB, and saved with the exact filename signature.jpg. 4. Packing Multi-Page Marksheets Under 256 KB for MahaDBT & College Admissions

Scholarship and centralized admission portals routinely ask students to merge their SSC marksheet, HSC marksheet, Leaving Certificate, and Caste or Income Certificate into a single PDF under 256 KB (or 300 KB for NTA NEET category uploads). Standard phone scanning apps save each page at 2–4 MB, producing an 8 MB PDF that fails immediately.

To keep 3 to 5 A4 pages legible under 256 KB, each page must be downscaled to roughly 760–960 px on its longest edge and encoded at calibrated JPEG quality (~45%–60%) before embedding into an A4 PDF container.
