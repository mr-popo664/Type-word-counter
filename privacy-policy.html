Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 · Domain: https://mohit-tools.com

1. Overview & Client-Side Data Processing

At Mohit Tools (https://mohit-tools.com), protecting your privacy is fundamental to how our tools are engineered. All calculators, text analyzers, image splitters, safe-zone previewers, and completion checklists execute 100% locally in your web browser using client-side JavaScript and standard browser APIs.

We do not upload, store, inspect, or transmit any text, IP addresses, resistor selections, images, or video files you place into our tools to any external application server. When you close your tab, transient workspace inputs in memory are cleared automatically.

2. Browser Local Storage (localStorage)

Certain optional conveniences use your browser’s localStorage purely on your device:

  • Theme Preference (mt_theme): Remembers whether you selected Light or Dark mode across visits.
  • Cookie Consent Choice (mt_cookie_consent): Records whether you selected "Accept" or "Essential Only" so the consent banner does not reappear on every page.
  • Checklist Progress (e.g., Leonida Tracker): Saves your completion checkboxes locally so your progress persists across reloads without creating an account.
Preference cleared. Reload the page to view the consent prompt again.

3. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure aggregate traffic patterns, page load performance, and which utilities are most helpful to visitors. Google Analytics collects anonymized technical metadata such as browser type, device viewport, referring website, and pages visited. IP anonymization is enabled where required, and no personally identifiable tool inputs are ever sent to analytics.

4. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Mohit Tools displays advertisements served by Google AdSense to keep every utility completely free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visit to mohit-tools.com and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google’s official Ads Settings page: https://adssettings.google.com. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookie use for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

5. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction—including residents of the European Economic Area (GDPR), the United Kingdom, and California (CCPA/CPRA)—you have specific rights regarding personal data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Request details on any personal information collected (note that Mohit Tools does not maintain user accounts or databases).
  • Right to Erasure & Correction: Request deletion of any correspondence you have sent via email, and clear browser-stored preferences directly via your browser settings.
  • Right to Opt Out of Personalized Ads: Select "Essential Only" on our cookie banner or configure ad personalization via adssettings.google.com.
  • Non-Discrimination: We never restrict access to any utility if you decline non-essential cookies.

6. Children’s Privacy & Contact Information

Mohit Tools does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise any statutory privacy rights, please contact us at mohitsonar13@gmail.com.
