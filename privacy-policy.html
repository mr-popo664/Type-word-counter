Privacy Policy

How Mohit Tools (mohit-tools.com) handles data, cookies, and third-party advertising.

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

1. Overview & Local Browser Processing

At Mohit Tools (accessible from https://mohit-tools.com), the privacy of our visitors is a top priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit our website and how it is used.

Your Tool Inputs Stay Private: All text analysis, word and character counts, and formatting operations on Mohit Tools run locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not transmit, store, log, or sell the text or captions you enter into our utilities.

2. Log Files

Like most standard websites, mohit-tools.com and our hosting infrastructure make use of log files. The information recorded in log files includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring/exit pages, and aggregate click counts. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is used strictly to analyze traffic trends, administer the site, and maintain security.

3. Cookies, Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

Mohit Tools uses cookies to store visitor preferences (such as cookie consent choices) and to support advertising that keeps our tools free.

  • Google AdSense: Google is a third-party vendor on mohit-tools.com and uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads to visitors based on their visits to this website and other websites across the internet.
  • How Google Uses Partner Data: Learn more about how Google collects and processes data when you use partner websites by visiting How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Mohit Tools, which are sent directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Mohit Tools has no access to or control over cookies used by third-party advertisers.

4. Analytics & Performance Monitoring

We use Google Tag Manager and web analytics tools to understand aggregate usage patterns—such as page views, device types, and page load performance—so we can improve the speed and usability of our utilities. You can disable cookies at any time through your individual browser settings.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location (including the European Economic Area, the UK, and California), you have specific rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

  • Request Access or Deletion: Request details about any personal data collected or ask that it be erased.
  • Opt Out of Personalized Advertising: Withdraw cookie consent or disable personalized ad tracking via Google Ads Settings.
  • Browser Cookie Controls: Block or clear browser cookies through your browser's privacy preferences.

6. Children's Information

Protecting children while using the internet is important to us. Mohit Tools does not knowingly collect any Personally Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such records.
