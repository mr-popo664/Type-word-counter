Technical Breakdown: Decimal GB vs. Binary GiB & PS5 OS Reserve

Why does a brand-new 825 GB PlayStation 5 show only 667.2 GB of free space out of the box? Two mathematical factors apply simultaneously. First, flash storage manufacturers measure capacity in base-10 gigabytes (1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes), while operating systems report base-2 gibibytes (1 GiB = 1,073,741,824 bytes). An 825 GB drive equals roughly 768.3 GiB raw.

Second, Sony reserves roughly 101 GB on the internal SSD for the PlayStation 5 operating system, system firmware snapshots, Kraken decompression scratch space, and the always-on 60-minute video capture ring buffer. The same base-2 conversion reduces a 1 TB M.2 NVMe expansion stick to approximately 931 GiB usable capacity.

Practical Storage Management Strategies

Cold-Store Inactive PS5 Titles

Move large single-player games onto an inexpensive USB 3.0 HDD for cold storage; copying back to the internal SSD takes minutes rather than hours of redownloading.

M.2 PCIe 4.0 Upgrade Planning

Ensure any internal M.2 NVMe upgrade supports PCIe Gen4 x4 (5,500 MB/s+ sequential read) with an integrated heatsink to match Sony’s specifications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does an 825 GB PS5 only have 667.2 GB of usable game space?

Storage manufacturers market drives in decimal gigabytes, whereas console operating systems measure capacity in binary gibibytes. Subtracting the PS5 OS and capture buffer leaves roughly 667.2 GB.

How much usable storage do the PS5 Slim (1 TB) and PS5 Pro (2 TB) offer?

The PS5 Slim 1 TB SSD provides approximately 848 GB of usable game space, while the PS5 Pro 2 TB SSD provides roughly 1,810 GB after OS formatting.

How big is Grand Theft Auto VI expected to be on PS5?

Based on 4K texture fidelity and dense Leonida streaming assets, analysts estimate 180 GB to 230 GB at launch. Use the slider to test conservative or heavier scenarios.

Can PS5 games run directly off an external USB drive?

No. Native PS5 games require internal NVMe SSD bandwidth. External USB drives can store PS5 games in cold storage or run PS4 titles directly.

Does adding an M.2 NVMe SSD combine with internal storage?

Yes, in terms of total installable library capacity. The PS5 lets you install and transfer native PS5 games seamlessly between internal and M.2 storage.

Why should I keep 20–30 GB of free SSD buffer even after installing GTA 6?

PlayStation updates and delta patches require temporary staging space so you do not have to uninstall titles every time a patch ships.
