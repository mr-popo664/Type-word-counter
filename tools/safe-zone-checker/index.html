How to Use the 9:16 Safe-Zone Overlay Checker

Short-form video platforms place interactive engagement buttons, account handles, audio tickers, and search bars directly over your 1080×1920 video canvas. Designing subtitles or product callouts inside Premiere Pro, CapCut, or DaVinci Resolve without verifying those overlays often leaves crucial hooks covered in the feed.

  1. Drop your 9:16 export: Select an exported MP4, MOV, or PNG storyboard frame from your computer—it stays locally in browser memory.
  2. Switch between TikTok, Reels & Shorts: Inspect each platform’s unique bottom caption height, right-side icon rail, and top navigation clearance.
  3. Verify inside the emerald safe boundary: Ensure all burned-in captions, sponsor logos, and focal faces remain strictly inside the dashed safe-area rectangle.

Technical Breakdown: 1080×1920 Dead Zones Across Platforms

While all three major vertical platforms share a 9:16 aspect ratio (1080 × 1920 px), their overlay geometry differs subtly across iOS and Android aspect ratios (such as 19.5:9 tall screens that crop left/right edges slightly).

On TikTok, the top Following/For You header occupies roughly 210 px, while a two-line caption plus music ticker claims 430 px from the bottom. Instagram Reels sits slightly higher at the bottom (400 px) when shopping or Remix tags appear, and YouTube Shorts reserves 360 px at the bottom for the channel subscribe bar and video title. Building a universal template with 220 px top, 430 px bottom, and 170 px right clearance guarantees zero obstruction across every network.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recommended safe zone for 1080x1920 vertical videos?

Keep essential text, subtitles, and logos at least 220px from the top, 420px from the bottom, 60px from the left, and 170px from the right edge on a 1080x1920 canvas.

Are my unreleased videos or client reels uploaded to a server?

No. Files are loaded exclusively into local browser memory via URL.createObjectURL, so unreleased edits never leave your computer.

Which platform has the largest bottom UI dead zone?

TikTok and Instagram Reels both occupy significant lower-third space when multi-line captions, music tickers, and shopping tags are active.

What video and image file formats are supported?

Any format natively supported by modern browsers, including MP4, WebM, MOV, PNG, JPG, and WebP.

Why should I also check the 4:5 center crop on Instagram Reels?

Instagram displays Reels inside both the 9:16 Reels tab and a cropped feed preview on your profile grid, so centering key headlines prevents top and bottom clipping.

Can I toggle the green safe-area guide frame on and off?

Yes. Use the Safe Margin Guide checkbox and UI Overlay Opacity slider to inspect both pixel boundaries and realistic in-feed readability.
