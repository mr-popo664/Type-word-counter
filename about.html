About Mohit Tools

Fast, lightweight, and privacy-first web utilities built for creators, writers, and everyday productivity.

Our Mission

Mohit Tools (mohit-tools.com) was created with a simple goal: everyday online utilities should be instant, clean, and free to use without mandatory sign-ups, bloated downloads, or cluttered interfaces.

Whether you are drafting an Instagram caption, checking word counts for an assignment, or planning storage space on your console, our tools are engineered to load rapidly on both desktop and mobile browsers and deliver accurate results in real time.

100% Browser-Based

All calculations and text transformations happen locally inside your browser with zero server lag.

Privacy First

We never store, log, or upload the text or data you enter into our tools.

Always Free

Every utility on Mohit Tools is accessible without accounts, paywalls, or hidden limits.

What We Build

We focus on practical single-purpose utilities designed around real workflows:

  • Advanced Word & Character Counter: Our flagship text analytics tool tracks words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, reading time, and speaking time in real time—complete with a visual 2,200-character Instagram caption progress bar, multi-platform limit checks (X/Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok), and instant text case converters.
  • Creator & Tech Utilities: Alongside our writing tools, we build specialized micro-utilities across the Mohit Tools network—such as our PS5 Storage Calculator and social media formatting helpers—to solve specific technical and content-creation tasks in seconds.
