About Mohit Tools

Welcome to Mohit Tools (mohit-tools.com), your go-to destination for fast, lightweight, and completely free web-based utilities designed to simplify everyday digital tasks.

Our Mission

Our goal is to build clean, responsive, and easy-to-use micro-tools that load instantly on any device without clutter, bloatware, or unnecessary distractions. Whether you are counting words for a social media caption or using our upcoming developer utilities, everything is built with performance and user experience in mind.

Who We Are

Mohit Tools is independently developed and maintained by Mohit Sonar, a technology enthusiast and developer passionate about creating practical web utilities that run right inside your browser.
